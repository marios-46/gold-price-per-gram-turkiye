Gold Pricing Explained

How Gold Prices Are Calculated Per Gram in Türkiye

The price shown for one gram of gold in Türkiye is the result of several connected calculations. Global gold prices, the Turkish lira exchange rate, the troy-ounce measurement, gold purity, and local market conditions all help determine the final figure seen by buyers and sellers.

Global benchmark Gold is commonly quoted internationally per troy ounce.
Local currency International values must be converted into Turkish lira.
Purity matters 24K, 22K, 21K and other grades contain different amounts of gold.

Why Is Gold Quoted Per Gram?

Gold can be measured using several weight units, but the international wholesale market commonly quotes precious metals by the troy ounce. Retail buyers, jewellery businesses and consumers often find grams easier to understand because grams are part of the metric system.

This means a gold price displayed per gram is not necessarily an independent market price. It can be a converted value derived from an international gold quotation and then adjusted for currency, purity and local trading conditions.

NIST identifies the troy ounce as approximately 31.103 grams and provides the more precise conversion factor of 31.1034768 grams per troy ounce.

The basic idea

Start with the international gold price, convert the troy-ounce quotation into a gram price, convert the currency into TRY when necessary, and then account for the purity of the gold being evaluated.

A jewellery shop may then apply its own pricing factors, so the calculated metal value and the amount paid for a finished jewellery item are not always identical.

The Core Formula Behind a Gram Gold Price

A simplified calculation can be understood as a sequence rather than a single mysterious number. If the international quotation is given in US dollars per troy ounce, the first step is to convert it into a dollar value per gram. The exchange rate can then convert that amount into Turkish lira.

TRY per gram ≈ (USD gold price per troy ounce ÷ 31.1034768) × USD/TRY This produces an approximate 24K-equivalent spot value before local premiums, spreads, workmanship or other retail adjustments.

NIST's precious-metals conversion information gives 31.1034768 grams as the conversion from one troy ounce. It also demonstrates that a price per gram can be converted back to a troy-ounce price by multiplying by the same factor.

Step 1: Start With the International Gold Price

Gold is traded internationally and market quotations are commonly expressed as a price for one troy ounce. This benchmark changes as market participants respond to supply, demand, currencies, interest rates, economic expectations and other financial conditions.

For a Turkish gram price calculation, the international quotation therefore provides the starting point. A website or calculator may obtain a market quotation in USD and then transform it into the required local unit.

Important distinction: A global spot calculation is a reference value for the metal itself. It should not automatically be interpreted as the exact amount a customer will pay for a finished jewellery product.

Step 2: Convert Troy Ounces Into Grams

The second step is the weight conversion. One troy ounce contains 31.1034768 grams. This is different from the ordinary avoirdupois ounce used for many everyday goods.

Understanding this conversion is especially useful when comparing international gold charts with Turkish websites that display prices per gram. A detailed explanation of the distinction can also be found in this guide to gold price per gram vs per ounce .

Step 3: Convert the Price Into Turkish Lira

If the original gold quotation is denominated in US dollars, the dollar-per-gram value has to be converted into Turkish lira. The relevant exchange rate can therefore have a substantial effect on the TRY value even if the international gold price itself has not changed.

Conceptually, the calculation looks like this:

International USD/gram value × USD/TRY exchange rate = TRY/gram

This is why a Turkish gold price can move for two different reasons: the international price of gold can change, or the relationship between the US dollar and Turkish lira can change. Both variables can influence the converted TRY value.

Step 4: Adjust for Gold Purity

Not every piece of gold contains the same percentage of pure gold. Karat indicates purity, with 24K representing the highest conventional karat grade used for gold products. Lower karat grades contain other metals in addition to gold.

24K

Usually treated as approximately 100% gold for simplified calculations, although actual fineness specifications can vary by product.

22K

Approximately 91.67% gold when represented as 22/24, making its intrinsic metal value lower per gram than a comparable 24K weight.

21K

Approximately 87.5% gold when represented as 21/24, so its pure gold content differs from 22K and 24K.

A useful way to understand this is to multiply the 24K-equivalent gram price by the purity fraction. For example, a simplified 21K calculation uses 21 ÷ 24 = 0.875. A dedicated 21K gold price page can therefore be useful when the goal is to examine that purity rather than simply looking at a 24K reference.

A Simple Illustrative Calculation

Consider an entirely illustrative example. Suppose an international quotation and exchange rate produce a hypothetical 24K reference value of 5,000 TRY per gram. The number is used only to demonstrate the mathematics and is not a current market quotation.

Example: 10 grams of 21K gold

Step 1: 24K reference = 5,000 TRY per gram
Step 2: 21K purity factor = 21 ÷ 24 = 0.875
Step 3: 21K reference value = 5,000 × 0.875 = 4,375 TRY per gram
Step 4: 10 grams × 4,375 TRY = 43,750 TRY

The resulting 43,750 TRY is an illustrative intrinsic-metal calculation. It should not be treated as a guaranteed jewellery selling price because retail products can contain additional pricing components.

Why the Shop Price Can Differ From the Calculated Value

One of the most important concepts for consumers is the difference between the theoretical value of the gold content and the final commercial price of an item.

1

Workmanship

Jewellery requires design, manufacturing and finishing. These services can contribute to the final retail price beyond the value of the raw gold.

2

Dealer Spread

Buyers and sellers may quote different prices. The difference helps cover business costs, market risk and operating margins.

3

Product Premium

Coins, bars, jewellery and other gold products can have different premiums depending on their form and market demand.

4

Other Charges

Depending on the transaction and product, applicable taxes, fees or other commercial costs may affect the amount ultimately paid.

What Makes the Turkish Gram Price Move?

The displayed TRY price is dynamic because several underlying variables can change. Watching only one factor can therefore provide an incomplete explanation of a daily movement.

Spot Price vs Jewellery Price

A spot-based calculation answers a relatively narrow question: what is the estimated market value of the underlying gold content? A jewellery quotation answers a broader commercial question: how much does this particular finished product cost or trade for?

These values should not automatically be expected to match. A ring, bracelet or necklace may contain stones, design work, manufacturing costs and a seller's commercial margin. On the other hand, a buyer purchasing used jewellery may apply deductions or a different spread because the transaction is based primarily on recoverable metal.

Practical rule: Always identify whether a quoted number represents spot metal value, a retail selling price, a dealer buying price, or a finished product price before comparing two gold quotations.

Why Purity and Weight Should Be Checked Together

Looking only at the number of grams can be misleading. Two pieces can have exactly the same weight but different gold content because their karat grades differ.

For example, 10 grams of 24K material and 10 grams of 18K material do not contain the same amount of pure gold. The first step in a meaningful comparison is therefore to identify both the weight and the purity.

A simplified intrinsic-value formula is:

Gold content value = weight × 24K gram reference price × purity factor

For a 22K item, a simplified purity factor is 22/24. For 21K, it is 21/24. More precise calculations can instead use the stated fineness of the product, such as 916.7 or 875, where appropriate.

Common Mistakes When Calculating Gold Prices

Using a regular ounce

Precious metals use the troy-ounce system. Substituting an ordinary ounce creates an incorrect gram conversion.

Ignoring the exchange rate

A USD-denominated gold quotation cannot be directly treated as a Turkish-lira price without applying the relevant currency conversion.

Comparing different karats

Comparing a 24K price directly with a 21K or 18K price without adjusting for purity can produce a misleading result.

Assuming spot equals retail

The calculated metal value does not necessarily include workmanship, dealer spreads, product premiums or other commercial charges.

How to Read a Gold Price Per Gram Correctly

When checking a Turkish gold-price page, start by identifying five pieces of information: the currency, weight unit, karat, timestamp and price type. A number without this context is difficult to compare accurately with another quotation.

  1. Confirm that the displayed currency is Turkish lira if you are comparing local TRY prices.
  2. Check whether the weight is one gram, one troy ounce, tola or another unit.
  3. Identify whether the quotation refers to 24K, 22K, 21K or another purity.
  4. Check when the underlying data was updated, especially when dealing with changing market prices.
  5. Determine whether the value is a spot/melt reference or a retail product quotation.

These checks make it much easier to compare different gold-price sources without accidentally comparing different measurements or purity grades.

Using a Gold Price Calculator

A calculator can simplify the process by performing the unit, currency and purity conversions automatically. However, the output should still be interpreted according to the methodology used by the calculator.

A transparent calculation normally identifies its gold-price source, currency conversion, weight conversion and purity factor. For example, some modern calculators explicitly describe their calculation as weight multiplied by spot price per gram and purity, while noting that retail premiums and workmanship are excluded.

This is useful because two calculators can display different results even when both are mathematically correct if they use different market snapshots, exchange rates, purity assumptions or commercial adjustments.

Why a Small Calculation Difference Can Matter

Gold is often bought or sold in quantities larger than one gram. A small difference in the calculated value per gram can therefore become more noticeable when multiplied by 10, 20, 50 or 100 grams.

The same principle applies to purity. A calculation that accidentally treats a 21K item as 24K can substantially overstate the theoretical gold content because the two materials do not contain the same proportion of pure gold.

For this reason, good calculations should show their inputs rather than simply presenting a final number.

A Quick Calculation Checklist

① Gold quotation

Identify the international gold price and determine whether it is quoted per troy ounce.

② Weight conversion

Divide the troy-ounce price by 31.1034768 to obtain the approximate 24K price per gram.

③ Currency conversion

Convert the dollar-based gram value into Turkish lira using the relevant USD/TRY rate.

④ Purity

Apply the appropriate karat or fineness factor when evaluating gold below the highest purity.

⑤ Weight

Multiply the per-gram value by the actual weight being evaluated.

⑥ Local factors

Consider spreads, premiums, workmanship and other applicable commercial costs separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the gold price per gram calculated in Türkiye?

A simplified calculation starts with the international gold price per troy ounce, divides it by 31.1034768 to obtain a per-gram value, converts the result into Turkish lira when needed, and adjusts for purity. Local commercial prices can then differ because of spreads, premiums and product-specific costs.

How many grams are in a troy ounce?

One troy ounce equals 31.1034768 grams. This is the conversion factor used for precious-metal calculations.

Why does the TRY gold price change when gold in USD changes?

The international gold quotation is often expressed in US dollars. When that value is converted into Turkish lira, changes in either the gold quotation or the USD/TRY exchange rate can affect the resulting TRY price.

Is 21K gold worth the same per gram as 24K gold?

Not on a pure-metal-content basis. A simplified 21K calculation uses a purity factor of 21/24, meaning it contains less gold by mass than an equivalent-weight 24K product.

Why can a jewellery shop quote a different price?

A jewellery quotation can include factors that are separate from the raw metal calculation, such as workmanship, product premium, dealer spread and other applicable commercial costs.

Should I compare gold prices only by the number shown per gram?

No. Check the currency, purity, timestamp, weight unit and price type first. A 24K spot reference and a finished 21K jewellery quotation are not directly equivalent measurements.

Can the gram price be calculated from the ounce price?

Yes. Divide the troy-ounce price by 31.1034768 to obtain the equivalent price per gram in the same currency. NIST documents this conversion for precious-metal sales.

Final Takeaway

Calculating the price of gold per gram in Türkiye is easier when the process is separated into individual steps. The international gold quotation provides the starting benchmark. The troy-ounce value is converted into grams, the currency is converted into Turkish lira, and the purity factor is applied when the gold is not 24K.

After that mathematical calculation, real-world prices can still differ because gold products are bought and sold through markets and businesses with their own spreads, premiums, workmanship and other commercial considerations.

The most reliable way to understand any displayed gold price is therefore to ask what the number represents, which unit it uses, which purity it assumes and whether it is a spot reference or a retail quotation. Once those details are clear, comparing gram prices in Türkiye becomes much more straightforward.
